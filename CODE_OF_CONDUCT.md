# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone. Participants are expected to be respectful, assume good faith, accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for users of the project.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption.

Report concerns to **conduct@audiospan.dev**. Maintainers will review reports promptly and confidentially and may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban participants who violate this code.

This document is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
